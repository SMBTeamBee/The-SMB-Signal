Lead story

Google's core update hit day 7 with its first real weekend swing, but trackers and Search Console are struggling to show it

Google's November 2026 core update started rolling out on Tuesday, Nov 10, and Google said it may take up to 2 weeks. Barry Schwartz reports that complaints about rankings, traffic and conversions picked up on Saturday, Nov 14, and that tracking tools in aggregate showed more volatility that day than on other days. He also calls the chatter more muted than for most core updates.

Schwartz suspects the quiet is partly because third-party tracking tools are struggling to monitor Google right now, and he notes that Search Console performance reporting has been laggy. Some of the charts people posted in reply to him predate the update, so treat the chatter as unconfirmed.

Glenn Gabe noted one example of why timing matters: Harvard started moving its health content to a new domain right before the update began. That is Gabe's observation, not a Google statement, but it shows how a big change during a rollout makes any movement hard to explain later.

Law firm angle

Hold redesigns, migrations and large content changes on client sites until the rollout ends, so the before and after stay clean. Keep logging calls and form fills alongside Search Console data, because a lagging report is not proof that nothing moved. Judge impact by comparing dates in Search Console after the update completes, and wait at least a full week after it ends before you act on a drop.

Google AI Mode drops the Gemini model label and widens Gemini 4 Carbon to AI Pro subscribers

AI Mode no longer shows which Gemini model is in use, and Gemini 4 Carbon, which first went to AI Ultra subscribers, is now live for AI Pro subscribers. Google's Rajan Patel confirmed both are intended and said Google is working out how to bring back a model indicator.

For law firmsDifferent models can return different answers and links. When you screenshot AI Mode for a client check, record the date and signed-in or signed-out state, since the model is no longer visible.

Google tests removing the AI Mode tab and the “Ask anything” box (unconfirmed)

Glenn Gabe saw a results page with no AI Mode tab and no “Ask anything” follow-up box under an AI Overview. Schwartz could not replicate it and says it may be one of Google's routine tests. This follows an earlier test that removed AI Mode buttons from the Google homepage.

For law firmsTreat it as a test. Keep your AI visibility checks consistent from month to month so a layout change does not look like a ranking change.

Bing tests a “Web Mode” tab that shows only web pages

Microsoft is testing a Web Mode tab in Bing that removes AI and Copilot answers and other features and leaves only web page results. Schwartz can see it in some browsers but says it is not fully live. Google has had a similar Web filter since May 2024.

For law firmsNo action. If it ships, people who pick it see classic results only, so normal Bing rankings are what count for them.

Today's concept Intermediate

Eligibility for AI features

AI search › AI Overviews and AI Mode › Indexed and snippet-eligible

A page can only appear as a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode if it is indexed and allowed to show a snippet in regular Google Search.

Why it matters: Google says there are no additional technical requirements and no special schema or AI text files needed. That makes eligibility a pass or fail check on basics you already control, and a page that fails it cannot be linked at all.

Legal example (hypothetical): a family law firm's divorce process page carries a nosnippet robots meta tag left over from an old template. The page ranks, but it is not eligible to be shown with a snippet, so it cannot be a supporting link in AI answers.

How to apply it:

  • Confirm the page is indexed (Search Console URL Inspection) and not blocked by noindex or robots.txt.
  • Look for nosnippet or a very low max-snippet value in the page source and in the X-Robots-Tag header.
  • Remove accidental restrictions left by plugins or templates, and keep deliberate ones only where the client has a reason.

Going deeper: Meeting the requirements does not guarantee crawling, indexing or showing, so eligibility is necessary, not sufficient. Google's snippet doc also describes data-nosnippet for blocking parts of a page from snippets. The AI features doc does not say how partial blocks affect AI links, so test before you rely on either answer. For measurement, Google reports AI features inside the Web search type in the Performance report.

Sources: Google: AI features and your website · Google: Control your snippets in search results

Tool tip · Census Reporter Beginner

Size up a client's market in two minutes with a place profile

censusreporter.org › search a place › profile page › Social section

Census Reporter is a free, independent site that makes American Community Survey (ACS, the Census Bureau's yearly survey) data easy to read. Each place profile shows a figure next to the same figure for the metro area, state and U.S., so you see right away whether a market is unusual.

  1. Go to censusreporter.org and type the client's city in the search box, then pick the matching place.
  2. On the profile page, scroll through the sections (Demographics, Economics, Families, Housing, Social) to the Social section.
  3. Read Foreign-born population and Veteran status. Each shows the place's rate, then the comparison to the metro area, state and U.S.
  4. Hover over a number to see its margin of error. If the margin is large next to the figure, treat it as a rough guide.
  5. Check the note under the place name for which ACS release the data comes from. The current site was updated with the 2024 ACS data in February 2026.

Law firm use case: an immigration firm can see how large the foreign-born population is in each city it serves before choosing which location pages to build out. A family law or estate planning firm can use veteran status and household figures to pick examples and local detail for page copy and GBP posts.

Pro move: use the site's Explore search to find a table by keyword, then download comparison data as CSV or Excel to compare several cities in one sheet.

Conversion corner

Put the next step in plain text, not only in a widget

As AI tools and agents read practice pages to answer for the firm, the text on the page is what they can work with. Write the call to action and what happens next in plain text near the top: “Call (555) 010-0100 for a free consultation. A paralegal will ask about your case, and an attorney reviews it the same day.” (hypothetical wording). Google's AI features guidance also lists making sure important content is available in textual form.
